🔥 Gesture-Controlled Fireball Between Hands
An interactive computer vision and graphics project that detects your hands in real-time, measures the distance between them, and dynamically renders a glowing, crackling fireball right between your palms.

The size, intensity, and sound effects of the fireball scale dynamically as you bring your hands together or pull them apart.

🚀 Features
Real-time Hand Tracking: Leverages ultra-low latency tracking to map both hands instantly via a standard webcam.

Dynamic Fireball Physics: The fireball expands, brightens, and flickers aggressively as you pull your hands apart, and shrinks to a concentrated spark when your hands are close.

Particle Effects: Randomized flame and smoke particles emit outward from the center point between your palms.

Visual Feedback: On-screen distance metrics and bounding boxes track your hands in real-time.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Language: Python 3.10+

Computer Vision: OpenCV, MediaPipe

Graphics & Rendering: Pygame (or PyOpenGL depending on your rendering choice)

Math Processing: NumPy
